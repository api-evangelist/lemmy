New NLnet funding for Lemmy The NLnet foundation has previously provided three funding rounds for Lemmy, which allowed Dessalines and Nutomic to work fulltime on the project. With this funding we were able to implement fundamental features such as federation, and to get Lemmy ready for production and to prepare for the huge migration of Reddit users. Now we have good news, a new round of funding was approved for a total of € 50.000.
