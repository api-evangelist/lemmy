Join-Lemmy Redesign and Funding Drive Written by @nutomic and @dessalines, 2023-10-31 Intro Some months have passed on since the Reddit blackout this June. It led to an explosive growth in Lemmy users, and lots of urgent work in scaling, bug fixes, user onboarding and more. Since then things have calmed down significantly, giving us breathing room and time to get more long-term work done.
