First release of LemmyBB, a federated bulletin board written in Rust Written by @nutomic, 2022-09-14 We are excited to announce the release of lemmyBB. This is an alternative frontend for Lemmy, based on the design of phpBB . It turns Lemmy from a link aggregator with upvotes and image previews into a traditional forum, or bulletin board.
